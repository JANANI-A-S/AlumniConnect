About AlumniConnect

AlumniConnect is a centralized alumni engagement and institutional development platform designed to strengthen the relationship between graduates and their alma mater. It brings together alumni records, campaign management, donation tracking, and financial visibility in a single, easy-to-use portal.

🎓

Alumni Network

Keep alumni connected through profiles, milestones, batches, and recognition programs that celebrate long-term impact.

🤝

Community Engagement

Support mentoring, event participation, and institutional collaboration through a shared digital experience.

💰

Fundraising & Giving

Track donations, campaign goals, and financial reports with a clear, transparent contribution ecosystem.

📊

Smart Insights

Monitor contributions, budgets, and engagement trends to make informed decisions for the institution.
